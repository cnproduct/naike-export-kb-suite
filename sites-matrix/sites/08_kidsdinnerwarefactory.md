## 3. 护城河与区隔壁垒 (Unique Moat)

**核心价值主张**:
> Ergonomically contoured for growing toddlers (ages 3+) with non-slip base geometry, anti-spill curved walls, and food-safe in-mold graphic technology that never peels into food.

**四大差异化护城河**:
1. **Curved**: Curved inner scoop rims designed to help children feed themselves with minimal spills
2. **Sub-surface**: Sub-surface in-mold labeling (IML) preventing baby cutlery scratch contact with printing pigments
3. **Smooth**: Smooth rounded edge finishing with zero micro-burrs exceeding child safety standards
